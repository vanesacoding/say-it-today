# Ten complete Let's Read books

Adds ten individually reviewed English books, taking the curated shelf from 73 to 83. Prior withdrawals remain in place. The ten new books appear first on the shelf so the trial batch is easy to find; the relative order of all existing records is preserved. All original source JPEG bytes and source English are retained; only HTML markup and whitespace are normalized. No source sentence is rewritten to make it easier. Chinese translations, one-action companion tips, three linked keywords per book and optional everyday activities are Jigu additions.

There are 105 reading pages and 102 narration jobs (5,283 characters). Three original wordless recap illustrations stay silent, with Chinese observation guidance. Book-end questions, publisher information and sponsor logos are excluded from the narrative sequence; every excluded original page and its reason is recorded. Their original full PDFs remain linked in the source sheet. Internal source page numbers sometimes skip values; ordered narrative content was checked against the official complete PDFs rather than assuming the API numbers are consecutive.

The source snapshot (`scripts/letsread-source-manifest.json`) pins official API URLs, raw response SHA256, English language ID, English and underlying source licenses, original HTML/text, source page IDs/order, image hashes/dimensions, exclusions, full original attribution and official PDF SHA256. All ten English versions and their underlying originals are CC BY 4.0, corroborated by the final attribution pages of the official PDFs. The full attribution, including translators where credited, is displayed in the app.

All artwork was inspected across complete source contact sheets. Selected subjects are household help, a birthday celebration, school shopping, counting, observation, growing corn, friendship and familiar occupations. Candidates with realistic spiders, a fierce lion holding raw meat, substantially wide spreads, overly long English or unnecessary frightening scenes were rejected. The freely downloadable Growing Reader decodables marked `Copyright`, and books marked NoDerivatives, were not imported. No ethnicity filter was used.

Phone art uses complete originals with object-fit:contain. Most pages are portrait; Shy Noy has a few A-series landscape pages (about 1.415:1) kept whole. Its source-specific geometry check permits up to 1.42:1; existing media rules and split-layout book checks remain unchanged. This admits the complete near-square scenes without cutting any character, while still rejecting 1.6:1 and 2:1 spreads. Previous/next controls remain fixed for the new books. Source English, images, narration and metadata of every existing record remain unchanged.

Validation includes immutable source/license checks; rejection of English rewrites, missing/reordered pages, changed images, removed attribution and changes to existing books; all 105 pages/30 keywords in the actual reader; all new pages at 320×568, 390×844, 430×932 and 1024×768; previous split-layout books; category/label consistency; fixed navigation; full media hashes, narration-to-text hashes and MP3 decoding before publication. Automated audio verification validates integrity and decodability, not a human listening review. The paid ledger records a request ID before submission, saves completed audio and stops on an uncertain result without an automatic paid retry.
