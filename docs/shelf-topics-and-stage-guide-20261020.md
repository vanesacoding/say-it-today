# Shelf topic consistency and compact stage guide

Topic filtering previously matched any entry in a book's themeIds, but every shelf card displayed only primaryTheme. Selecting 心情与朋友 could therefore show I like to read! labelled 日常生活, or Sleepy Mr Sloth labelled 动物与自然. The shelf now filters by primaryTheme, using the same category as its visible label. Secondary theme metadata is retained. All four topic filters and their stage intersections are checked against the visible card labels.

The stage guide uses three compact rows with 13px labels, 12px descriptions, restrained dividers and 11px supporting text. Its title remains 15px. The guide keeps its explanation that stages have no unlocking gate and can be freely switched.

Withdraw My grandma. (0130, 奶奶和我) and How Tembe spends her day in life (0344, 滕贝的一天, requested as 藤贝的一天). The shared curation exclusion applies across shelf, recommendations, history, favourites and vocabulary. The active shelf now has 73 books. Source English, artwork and all audio remain unchanged.

Validation: full application checks and build, plus Chromium checks at 320×568, 390×844, 430×932 and 1024×768 for matching category labels, compact guide typography, navigation and all 37 recently added mobile reading pages.
