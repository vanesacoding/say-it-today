# Two complete Book Dash books for mobile shared reading

Adds Little Goat (小山羊, 故事进阶 / 动物与自然, Charlotte, British English) and Can we eat a rainbow? (我们能吃彩虹吗？, 短句共读 / 颜色与发现, Knightley, British English). The curated shelf grows from 73 to 75; How do you sleep? remains deferred. All previously withdrawn books stay withdrawn.

Both books retain all 12 narrative spreads. Covers, ownership pages, credits and endpapers are attributed but excluded from the story sequence. The source JPEG bytes are unchanged and SHA256 pinned. Phone viewports focus complete subjects or split independent scenes into successive pages. Two-character scenes use overlapping, stacked panels where necessary to avoid cutting the figures. Every reading page opens its complete original spread and complete source English in a dismissible sheet; closing returns to the same page.

Little Goat becomes 15 reading pages; Can we eat a rainbow? becomes 22. The 37 audio jobs are exclusively new pages (2,600 characters). Each book uses one fixed native British voice, speech rate -5, calm picture-book narration. Existing text, illustration, voice and audio records remain byte-for-byte unchanged in the release plan. The production ledger records request IDs before submission and stops after uncertain outcomes rather than retrying paid requests.

English is transcribed from the public Book Dash gallery, with line breaks normalized only. For example, Little Goat's “She just walked along looking for the sweetest grass.” has no inserted comma, while the unusual original “Where are you, Little Goat,” remains unchanged. Source spread transcripts and exact reading-page mappings live in scripts/library-release-books-phone.json. Chinese translations, one-action companion prompts, three source-linked keywords and an optional everyday activity are added by Jigu.

Validation covers the 24 source spreads, all 37 mobile pages, full-original return navigation, source-linked words, narration fingerprints, rejection of changed existing books and invalid framing, plus phone/tablet layout at 320×568, 390×844, 430×932 and 1024×768. New-book previous/next controls remain fixed at the bottom; the reader title remains sticky. Production checks validate every generated MP3 and the full shelf before deployment. Automated audio verification checks integrity and decodability, not a human listening review.
